How to stay safe

  • Keep secrets secret. Never share passwords, one-time codes, PINs, or recovery phrases with someone who contacted you.
  • Don’t rush or click. Avoid unexpected links and attachments. Visit the company’s official app or type its known website address yourself.
  • Verify urgent requests independently. Hang up and contact the person or organization using a phone number you already trust—not one supplied in the message or call.
  • Pause before paying. Be cautious about urgent requests for gift cards, cryptocurrency, wire transfers, or secrecy.

Scams change quickly. Alerts are informational; verify advice with the linked official agency.
